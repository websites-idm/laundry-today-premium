IMPORTANT BRANDING RULES

 Keep the overall visual language similar to MyLaundry (clean, rounded, minimal, service-focused), but do not copy their layout.

 Use the Laundry Today logo everywhere appropriate.

 Match the logo's identity: blue backgrounds, white content areas, orange accent strips and buttons.

 All major section headings (Services, About, Pricing, Reviews, Contact) should use white uppercase text inside rounded orange strips, inspired by the logo's orange garments.

 Use realistic photography with blue color grading to maintain brand consistency.

 Create a premium, trustworthy experience that feels like a modern technology-enabled laundry company rather than a traditional local laundry shop.
